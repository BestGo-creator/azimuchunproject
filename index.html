A school built on freedom with responsibility

Disciplined School is a private educational institution in Yunusabad District, Tashkent, with approximately 500 students in grades 1-9.

  • ~500 students
  • 1-9 grades
  • 5 floors

Students are trusted to manage their own work and are held to account for the results. After grade 9, they can follow career-oriented pathways into employment without needing a university degree.

Discipline system

Students are grouped into classes according to their discipline and behaviour. Every month, a student can move to a higher or lower group depending on their conduct. Repeated serious disciplinary problems are reviewed by the school administration. Dismissal is possible only after a fair review.

  1. Higher group
  2. Current group
  3. Lower group
  4. Reviewed monthly. Movement goes both ways.
